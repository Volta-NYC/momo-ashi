Our story

Behind the paw

It all started with a gentle press.

Momo Ashi turns the joyful shape of a cat or dog's paw into food you can hold: handcrafted drinks, savory snacks, sweet toast, and warm Wamochi made for everyday comfort.

"We wondered what comfort would taste like"

Inspired by the irresistible joy of gently pressing a paw, Momo Ashi became a place where playful ideas, handcrafted flavors, and everyday comfort come together, leaving a little paw print in the day.

The origin of Wamochi

Turning comfort into flavor

Wamochi is our take on the mochi waffle: lightly crisp outside and soft, satisfying, and chewy inside.

Every Wamochi is made fresh using our custom paw-shaped waffle press, creating golden edges and our signature paw at the center.

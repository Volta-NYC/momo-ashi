Our Story

Your favorite
bubble tea spot

Momo Ashi is a beloved bubble tea destination with three convenient locations across New York — Bayside, Flushing, and Woodside.

Specializing in refreshing bubble tea and delightful Asian-inspired snacks, we offer a cozy retreat for tea lovers and food enthusiasts alike. Our menu features handcrafted beverages with premium ingredients, alongside savory rice wraps and sweet toast creations that pair perfectly with your drink.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick pick-me-up or settling in for a relaxing break, Momo Ashi combines quality, comfort, and a touch of creativity in every cup and bite.

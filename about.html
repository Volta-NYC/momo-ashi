Our story

Behind the paw

It all started with a gentle press.

Momo Ashi turns playful ideas, handcrafted flavors, and everyday comfort into snacks and drinks that leave a little paw print in your day.

"We wondered what comfort would taste like"

Inspired by the simple joy of a paw-shaped press, Momo Ashi became a place where cozy food, expressive characters, and tea-shop rituals all meet.

The origin of Wamochi

Turning comfort into flavor

Wamochi is our take on the mochi waffle: lightly crisp outside and soft, satisfying, and chewy inside.

Every Wamochi is made fresh with golden edges and a signature paw at the center.

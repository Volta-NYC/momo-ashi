Our story

Behind
the paw

It all started with a gentle press.

Seven flavors. One signature paw

“We wondered
what comfort
would taste like”

Inspired by the irresistible joy of gently pressing a cat’s or dog’s paw, we set out to turn that feeling into something you could taste.
Momo Ashi is a place where playful ideas, handcrafted flavors, and everyday comfort come together—leaving a little paw print in your day.

The origin of Wamochi

Turning comfort
into flavor

Wamochi is our take on the mochi waffle—lightly crisp on the outside and soft and satisfyingly chewy on the inside.

Every Wamochi is made fresh using our custom paw-shaped waffle press, creating golden, crisp edges and our signature paw at the center.

It’s this contrast of textures that makes Wamochi special: a warm, delicate crunch gives way to a dense, chewy mochi center.
